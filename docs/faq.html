Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No watermark, no limits on minutes or clips, no account. Clipshaper runs on your own computer, so it costs us nothing when you use it. If it helps you, you can tip us on Ko-fi.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. The app does everything on your computer. The web version runs inside your browser tab: the video never leaves it. The only downloads are the AI models, once, from Hugging Face, and the browser keeps them for next time.

How does it pick the best moments?

It writes down every word with timings, then a small AI model reads the transcript like an editor would. It looks for a line that grabs attention on its own, a moment that makes sense without the rest of the video, and a payoff. Each clip gets a score out of 10, and the best ones come first. You can trim any clip to the word.

What kind of videos work best?

Anything with talking: podcasts, interviews, streams, lectures, sermons, vlogs, reaction videos. It needs speech to find moments, so music videos and silent footage won't work.

Can I paste a YouTube, TikTok or Twitch link?

In the app, yes: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, Twitch, Facebook, Vimeo and many more. In the browser, no, because browsers don't let one website download videos from another. Only use videos you own or have permission to use.

Web or app: which should I use?

Try the web version first, nothing to install. The app is faster, uses bigger and smarter models, works in about 99 languages, pastes links, and exports in full quality in the background. The web version needs a recent Chrome or Edge for the smart picks.

How does the vertical framing work?

Clipshaper finds faces in each clip and moves a 9:16 window to follow the speaker. It holds still when it can and jumps on camera cuts, like a camera operator would. You can switch any clip to Centre or Whole frame (the full shot over a blurred background).

What do I need?

Web: Chrome or Edge on a computer from the last few years. App: a Mac with Apple silicon (macOS 12 or later) or Windows 10/11, 8 GB of memory, and about 3 GB of space for the models.
