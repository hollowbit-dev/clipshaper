Mac

macOS 12 or later, Apple silicon (M1 or newer).

Download for Mac
Mac says it can't check the app?

Clipshaper isn't sold through the App Store, so the first time, right-click the app in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. After that it opens normally.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

Download for Windows
Windows shows a blue warning?

That's SmartScreen, shown for new apps without a paid certificate. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
